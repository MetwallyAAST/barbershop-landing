Our story

Two chairs, one obsession with detail.

Fade & Blade started in a single back room with two chairs, a battered record player and a simple promise: no one leaves until it's right. Fifteen years later that promise hasn't changed — only the room got bigger.

We blend the discipline of classic barbering with the speed and edge of modern styling. Every barber on our floor is trained in-house across four hundred hours before they ever touch a paying head of hair.

  • Sanitised, single-use blades & fresh linens
  • Complimentary drinks — espresso, IPA or bourbon
  • Free style check-ups within two weeks of your cut
